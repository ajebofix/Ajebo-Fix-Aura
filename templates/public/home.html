THE PROBLEM

A repair ends. The vehicle's story doesn't.

Service documents get scattered. Problems are explained more than once. Past decisions lose their context. The result is fragmented information around an asset you rely on every day.

Aura helps bring that history forward so the next conversation begins with context, not guesswork.

ANOTHER STANDARD OF CARE

One connected view. More informed decisions.

01 / RECORD

Vehicle memory

Keep service history, recorded mileage, observations and care decisions together with their available sources.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Health awareness

Make sense of recorded concerns and advisor-reviewed information—without mistaking an observation for a diagnosis.

03 / COORDINATE

Professional care

Follow consultations, assessments, approved care actions and their documented progression.

04 / CONTINUE

Long-term context

Preserve useful history across future reviews and service decisions instead of starting over each time.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before you begin.

Can I register immediately?

Private client onboarding is currently controlled. Existing clients and invited drivers can continue using their authorised access.

Does Aura diagnose my vehicle?

No. It organises recorded information and care workflows. Professional mechanical diagnosis requires an appropriate assessment.

Does Aura track my car live?

No live vehicle connection is implied. Mileage and health information reflect available records and observations, with their limitations.

Is Aura the same as Ajebo Fix?

Ajebo Fix is the automotive practice behind Aura. Aura provides a connected technology experience for documented vehicle care.
